About us.

A small publishing house. A few books, each one closely looked after.

What we do

Oroboro Press publishes memoir and fiction as eBooks and audiobooks, in Italian and English. It was born in 2026 from a friendship and a book: Crumbs by Nicola Nuti.

We follow every book from start to finish: editing, cover, voice, translation, and release on Amazon, Spotify and Audible, and here on our site.

Our story

Francesco was born in Italy, but he truly discovered books in San Francisco. He had come for a job in tech, and on Sundays he would drive into the city, to North Beach, to City Lights, Lawrence Ferlinghetti's bookstore. Upstairs, in the poetry room, he learned something he never forgot: a small publisher can change what the world reads.

Then came the sea. In 2018 he and his wife Yuka set sail from South Africa aboard the catamaran Oroboro on a voyage around the world. In the middle of an ocean, between one night watch and the next, what else is there to do but read? The cabin filled up with books swapped in harbours, read twice and handed on to other crews.

Crossing the Atlantic from South Africa to Brazil, Francesco was reading the letters of Amerigo Vespucci, who had sailed along the same coasts five hundred years earlier. Vespucci was a Florentine, and his letters, printed and read all over Europe, persuaded a mapmaker to give his name to an entire continent. America is called America because of a Florentine, and a book.

The ouroboros, oroboro in Italian, is the snake that bites its own tail, an ancient symbol of renewal: a circle that ends where it begins. After three oceans and half the world, the first book from Oroboro Press is by a Florentine, Nicola Nuti, and it is about Florence. The circle closes where it started.

Yuka designed the logo: a wind rose inside the snake's circle, its head pointing north.

How we work with authors

  • The author always has the last word on the text. We suggest, the author decides.
  • Rights stay with the author. We publish on the author's behalf.
  • We choose prices, covers and platforms together.
  • Every book can have a second life in English, translated by us.

Publisher

Francesco Pugliano

Italian-born, he spent many years in big tech in Silicon Valley, including at eBay. Since 2018 he has been sailing around the world by catamaran with his wife Yuka; today they live between Greece, California and Japan.

He writes about their travels on sailingoroboro.com and builds apps for people who live on boats. At Oroboro Press he handles editing, English translation, eBooks and audiobooks.

Our first author

Nicola Nuti

Born in Florence in 1960, journalist and art critic. He started out in the 1980s writing for La Città and editing texts for art catalogues. He has written studies and monographs on Classical Abstractionism (Astrattismo Classico), the movement founded in Florence in 1950 by a group of painters including his father, Mario Nuti (1923–1996).

In Crumbs he gathers, in twenty-two short and wry memories, his encounters with the painters, writers and critics of Florence over the last sixty years, from Vasco Pratolini to Karel Appel. The cover shows Il cane, a painting by Mario Nuti. He is now writing his next book, L'inatteso.
