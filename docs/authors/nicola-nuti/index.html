Nicola Nuti

Journalist and art critic.

Biography

Born in Florence in 1960, Nicola Nuti is a journalist, consultant and art critic. He started out in the 1980s writing for La Città and editing texts for art catalogues.

He has written studies, articles and monographs on Classical Abstractionism (Astrattismo Classico), the movement that grew up in Florence in 1950 around a group of painters including his father, Mario Nuti (1923–1996).

In Crumbs he gathers in twenty-two memories his encounters with the painters, writers and critics of Florence over the last sixty years. He is now writing L'inatteso.
